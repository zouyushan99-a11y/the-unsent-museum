# Trilingual acceptance record — 22 November 2026

## Scope and result

This is a qualified acceptance record, not a declaration that every feature and translation is perfect.

The live site was inspected through Chrome in English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. All 69 personal and 20 collective dossier routes were opened in each language. Checks waited for the rendered DOM after navigation. Personal dossiers showed narrative text, curatorial analysis and three reader questions; collective dossiers showed narrative and analysis. Source links were present. A separate local JSDOM suite rendered 122 routes in three languages (366 checks), including failure/success handling for feedback and language switching with an unfinished form.

Desktop checks used a 1363 CSS-pixel viewport. Narrow-layout checks used browser zoom to obtain a 340 CSS-pixel viewport. All 122 routes were visited in all three languages at both widths. No blank main content or whole-document horizontal overflow was observed in the recorded checks. This is responsive-layout evidence, not physical-phone or touchscreen testing.

## Limits and work not verified

- No authenticated end-to-end test of login, password recovery, author story publication/deletion, private draft synchronization, curator review or moderation was performed in this browser session. The presence of database functions does not establish the entire authorized-user workflow.
- The new external translation provider's quota, billing, renewal and end-to-end authenticated publishing were outside this acceptance run.
- External archival images depend on third-party servers. Captions, source links and fallback behavior were inspected, and Kafka's manuscript image loaded in the live browser; this run does not certify every external image at every network location.
- Physical phones, touch gestures, iOS Safari, Android Chrome, tablet rotation and assistive-technology operation were not tested. Browser zoom was used for narrow-layout checks.
- Text scans detect untranslated fragments and structural omissions; they are not a word-by-word scholarly review of all historical interpretations, sources or translation nuance. Proper names, research titles, scientific abbreviations and explained historical terms are legitimate original-language exceptions.
- Browser console also contained extension-generated metadata errors, which were distinguished from website errors. The observed site error on direct Feedback entry was repaired and retested.
